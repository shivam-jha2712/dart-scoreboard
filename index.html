HOW TO PLAY

Rules

Everything your crew needs before the first dart lands.

🎯

Classic 301 / 501 / 701

Start from the chosen score and subtract the total of each player’s three-dart turn. First player to reach exactly zero wins.

  • Enter 0–180 for each dart.
  • If a turn scores more than the remaining score, it is a bust and the score returns to where it started.
  • Use Undo if a round was entered incorrectly.
⚾

Baseball Darts

Play 9 innings. Every player gets one turn per inning and records the number of runs they score.

  • There is no maximum official run total; this app caps one entry at 50 to keep input sensible.
  • After inning 9, the highest total wins.
  • Great for casual groups and mixed skill levels.
📱

Live QR Room

The host creates a room and shows the QR code. Friends scan it to open the same scoreboard on their phones.

  • The host controls scoring.
  • Phones joining the room are viewers.
  • Keep the host tab open while playing.
💡

House rules

Use the app as your scoreboard, then agree on your own checkout, double-out, handicap or bull rules before starting.
